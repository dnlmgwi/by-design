## Course Overview

This intensive master class is designed to take you from fundamental design concepts to professional-grade execution. With a focus on practical, hands-on learning, you'll develop a comprehensive skill set applicable to real-world design challenges.

By the end of this course, you will be able to:
- Apply core design principles to create effective visual communications
- Use industry-standard tools like Figma with confidence
- Develop brand identities from strategy to execution
- Create professional designs for both print and digital media
- Understand the business side of design including client communication
- Build a portfolio of work that showcases your skills

## Course Structure

### Module 1: Design Fundamentals (4 Weeks)

#### Week 1: Introduction to Design Principles
You'll learn the fundamental elements and principles of design that form the foundation of all visual communication. These concepts will guide your design decisions throughout your career.

**Topics:**
- Elements of design: line, shape, color, texture, space
- Principles of design: balance, contrast, emphasis, rhythm, unity
- Visual perception and Gestalt principles
- Design history and contemporary trends

**Practical Exercise:** Visual analysis of effective designs

#### Week 2: Mastering the Pen Tool
The pen tool is essential for creating precise vector graphics. You'll develop the skills to create and manipulate vector shapes and paths with confidence.

**Topics:**
- Introduction to vector graphics
- Pen tool anatomy and functionality in Figma
- Anchor points, handles, and paths
- Advanced pen tool techniques: corners, curves, and precision drawing

**Practical Exercise:** Create complex shapes and illustrations using only the pen tool

**Practice Resource:** [Bezier Game - Pen Tool Practice](https://bezier.method.ac/)

#### Week 3: Color Theory and Psychology
Color is a powerful communication tool. You'll learn how to use color effectively to convey messages, evoke emotions, and create visually appealing designs.

**Topics:**
- Color models (RGB, CMYK, HSB)
- Color psychology and emotional responses
- Creating effective color schemes
- Color harmony and contrast
- Accessibility considerations in color choices

**Practical Exercise:** Develop multiple color palettes for different brand personalities

**Useful Tools:**
- [Coolors](https://coolors.co/)
- [Adobe Color](https://color.adobe.com/)
- [Color Contrast Checker](https://colourcontrast.cc/)

#### Week 4: Typography Fundamentals
Typography is both an art and science. You'll learn how to select, pair, and arrange type to enhance readability and communicate effectively.

**Topics:**
- Typeface anatomy and classification
- Font pairing principles
- Hierarchy and readability
- Typesetting best practices
- Responsive typography

**Practical Exercise:** Create a typographic poster with effective hierarchy

**Useful Tools:**
- [Type Scale](https://typescale.com/)
- [Google Fonts Knowledge](https://fonts.google.com/knowledge)

---

### Module 2: Visual Communication (2 Weeks)

#### Week 5: Layout Theory and Composition
Learn how to arrange design elements effectively to guide viewers through your content in a meaningful way.

**Topics:**
- Grid systems and structure
- White space and visual breathing room
- F-pattern and Z-pattern in layout design
- Responsive design principles
- Golden ratio and other composition frameworks

**Practical Exercise:** Create multiple layouts for print and digital platforms

#### Week 6: Brand Identity Development
Discover the process of creating cohesive brand identities that communicate a brand's personality and values effectively.

**Topics:**
- Brand strategy fundamentals
- Logo design principles and the 7 types of logos
- Visual identity systems
- Brand style guides
- Consistent brand application

**Practical Exercise:** Create a comprehensive brand identity for a fictional company

---

### Module 3: Professional Practice (3 Weeks)

#### Week 9: Project Workflow Management
Learn to manage the design process efficiently from initial brief to final delivery.

**Topics:**
- Design process and project phases
- Time management for designers
- Client brief development
- Feedback integration methods
- Version control and file organization

**Practical Exercise:** Develop a project plan and timeline for a comprehensive design project

#### Week 10: Client Communication
Develop the skills to communicate effectively with clients, present your work professionally, and handle feedback constructively.

**Topics:**
- Presentation techniques
- Explaining design decisions
- Handling revisions and feedback
- Pricing strategies and proposals
- Contract essentials for designers

#### Week 11: Design Ethics and Industry Standards
Understand the ethical responsibilities of designers and the standards that guide professional practice.

**Topics:**
- Copyright and fair use
- Accessibility standards (WCAG)
- Sustainable design practices
- Design ethics and inclusivity
- Staying current in the industry

## Study Tips

### For Success in This Course
1. **Practice regularly** – Design skills improve with consistent practice
2. **Seek inspiration** – Analyze designs you admire to understand what makes them effective
3. **Embrace feedback** – View critiques as opportunities for growth
4. **Iterate** – Great design rarely happens on the first try
5. **Take risks** – Don't be afraid to experiment with new techniques and approaches
6. **Ask questions** – If you're unsure about something, chances are others are too

### Effective Study Habits
1. **Review class materials** before and after each session
2. **Take visual notes** – Sketch concepts to reinforce your understanding
3. **Form study groups** with classmates to discuss ideas and challenges
4. **Apply concepts** to personal projects to deepen your learning
5. **Schedule dedicated practice time** for technical skills like the pen tool
